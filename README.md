# Hi, I'm Noelani 👋

Coastal & water resources researcher working at the intersection of **hydrodynamic modeling**, **tidal-marsh restoration**, and **tribal climate adaptation**. PhD candidate in Water Resources Engineering at the University of New Mexico, based in the Greater Seattle area.

## What I'm working on

- **PhD Chapter 1 — Tidal-marsh restoration suitability.** Tier 3 post-processing of D-Flow FM output using EOF decomposition, hierarchical clustering, and slack-water state identification to evaluate how engineered channel geometry shapes phase-resolved low-energy marsh habitat.
- **PhD Chapter 2 — Adaptive marsh geometry.** A candidate-driven workflow that couples parameterized channel-network generation, morphostatic feasibility gates, D-Flow FM simulation, and treed Gaussian process sampling to search for channel configurations that improve seed-settlement conditions.
- **Tribal Climate Adaptation Specialist** at the [UW Climate Impacts Group](https://cig.uw.edu/) — building a Washington Tribal climate-adaptation planning inventory and technical-assistance referral workbook, and supporting the Washington State of Knowledge marine chapter.
- **Science Advisor**, [SeaDoc Society](https://www.seadocsociety.org/blog/new-seadoc-society-science-advisors) (Salish Sea marine ecosystem health).
